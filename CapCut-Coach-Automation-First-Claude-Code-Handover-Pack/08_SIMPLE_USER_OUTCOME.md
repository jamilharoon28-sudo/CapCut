# What You Get — Simple Version

CapCut Coach becomes your local first editor. You give it a script and the large raw-footage folder. It sorts the material, understands the script, finds the useful takes, creates captions and graphics, and builds three versions:

- **Clean** — safe, clear and faithful to the script.
- **Enhanced** — the recommended version with a few tasteful improvements.
- **Bold** — more experimental ideas for you to consider.

You watch short previews and press one button: choose this, faster, calmer, change the hook or try again. Coach then renders the final MP4 on your Mac and checks it for black frames, missing captions, wrong size, audio problems and missing required script points.

You do not need a CapCut project, previous finished videos or CapCut Pro. If you want to touch something up, **Open in CapCut Free** prepares the MP4, captions and assets and Coach tells you the next step in plain language.

It learns over time from which version you chose, what you changed and the final videos you approved. Finished examples can accelerate that learning, but they are optional.

For a typical 2GB shoot, expect the computer to need temporary working room for proxies, audio, previews and caches. With 77GB currently available, the recommended default is one active project, a warning below 30GB free and a hard pause before falling below 15GB. Heavy stages run one at a time and use the M2's media hardware where possible; this is normal work for the chip and should not “kill” it.

Realistic help depends on footage quality and format. A sensible measured target is roughly 45–65% of the hands-on job on the first zero-example videos, 60–75% after about five reviewed jobs and 70–85% for repeatable formats after roughly 10–20 good outcomes. Those are evaluation targets, not promises.

The required application path costs $0 extra: it uses free local components and the Claude Max plan you already have. If the included Claude allowance is unavailable, it falls back to deterministic local editing or pauses that optional judgment stage; it never buys overage.

The important boundary is simple: Coach may automatically analyse and render, but you approve publishing, deletion, claims and any experimental CapCut project action. It never deletes anything from CapCut Cloud/Space or Google Drive.
