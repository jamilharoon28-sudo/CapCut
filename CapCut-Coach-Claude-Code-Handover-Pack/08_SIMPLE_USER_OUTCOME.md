# What CapCut Coach Will Do — Simple Version

CapCut Coach will be your personal editing assistant on your Mac.

You give it raw footage. It learns the main style you like from your editor's previous raw and finished videos. It then does most of the slow preparation automatically.

## For every new video

CapCut Coach will:

1. Find and organise the footage.
2. Read your Word/PDF script pack and separate it into reels and requested shots.
3. Write out everything that was said where relevant.
4. Find the best hook, shots and strongest takes.
5. Remove mistakes, repeated attempts and unnecessary pauses.
6. Create different short versions.
7. Clean the voice and prepare captions where needed.
8. Suggest or add the usual zooms, B-roll and CTA; music is optional.
9. Prepare Faithful and Enhanced first cuts.
10. Open the safest available version in CapCut Premium.
11. Tell you exactly what to click next, one step at a time.
12. Check the video after you export it.
13. Learn from the changes you made.
14. Free Coach's temporary local space without deleting anything from CapCut Cloud or Google Drive.

## What you will see

You will not see code or technical settings. The main buttons will be:

- **Create Edit**
- **Open in CapCut**
- **Show My Next Step**
- **Review Export**

If you are confused in CapCut, press **I'm Stuck**. Coach will explain the next action in simple language.

## What remains for you

You will still:

- approve the hook and message;
- decide whether the video feels right;
- use any final CapCut Premium effects that cannot be safely automated;
- correct anything you dislike;
- approve the final export.

## How much it may help

For repeatable talking-head and educational videos, the long-term goal is for Coach to handle most of the repetitive work and reduce hands-on editing time substantially. The first release must prove at least a 40% median saving on unseen test videos before claiming success.

A typical workflow could change from several hours of sorting, cutting, captioning and checking to approximately 30–90 minutes of review and Premium finishing for a straightforward short reel. Complex advertisements will still take longer.

## What it should cost you

The application itself is designed around free/open-source local tools and your existing CapCut Premium and Claude subscriptions. It must not require hosting, a database service or another monthly subscription. Extra paid Claude usage is off by default, and the app must stop and explain the situation if the included allowance is unavailable or exhausted.

It can connect to a specific streamed Google Drive folder or local folder. Large files are downloaded/staged only while needed, processed one at a time and then removed from Coach's local workspace. Cloud originals remain untouched.

## What it does to your M2 Mac

It will use the processor during transcription and preview rendering, so the Mac may become warm. It is designed to run only one heavy task at a time, use smaller proxy files, pause during a CapCut export and stop before disk space becomes unsafe. Normal processing should not damage the chip; macOS and Apple silicon also manage temperature automatically.

## What it will protect

- It will not change your only CapCut project.
- It will never remove raw footage without showing the exact local files/size and receiving explicit approval.
- It will make backups before supported CapCut changes.
- It will stop direct automation if CapCut updates to an untested version.
- It will keep cloud originals in the cloud and use temporary local working copies only when needed.
- It will not secretly spend extra Claude money.

The goal is simple: **Coach completes the boring first 60–80%, CapCut Premium gives you the finishing tools, and the remaining work teaches you how to become a better editor.**
